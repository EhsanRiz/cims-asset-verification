Who we are

A Lesotho-grown digital and climate solutions firm

4D Climate Solutions (4D) was founded in 2018 by three climate action enthusiasts in Lesotho, driven by a mission to empower communities to engage in solutions to combat climate change and strengthen their resilience. What sets 4D apart is how we deliver: cutting-edge digital technology combined with deep community engagement, ensuring sustainable and lasting impact.

Over seven years, digitization has become the backbone of our work. We have moved government and development programs from paper registers, spreadsheets, and manual verification to secure, auditable, real-time digital systems — designed for low-bandwidth environments, local languages, and field conditions. Today 4D operates live production platforms for public-sector programs, including the compensation information system serving the Lowlands Water Development Project.

What we do

Digitization capabilities

Six capability pillars cover the full lifecycle of a government digitization program — from system design and data migration to field rollout, training, and long-term operation.

01

Government information systems & registries

Design and development of secure management information systems for public programs: beneficiary and asset registries, compensation management, grievance redress, monitoring & evaluation, and administrative workflows. Systems include role-based access control, approval chains, full audit trails, and print-ready official forms.

02

Field data collection & digitization of paper records

Offline-first mobile and web applications for surveys, censuses, verification exercises, and community consultations (pitsos, FGDs, KIIs) — with GPS capture, photo and document digitization, and real-time dashboards for supervisors. Built to work on 2G/3G networks and sync automatically when connectivity returns.

03

AI & smart data systems

AI-powered decision support and advisory platforms through our dedicated 4D AI division (ai.4dcs.co.za): retrieval-augmented (RAG) advisory systems, AI vision for real-time monitoring, and predictive analytics — engineered for low-bandwidth environments, local languages, and African realities, using Anthropic Claude AI.

04

Secure cloud infrastructure & data protection

Cloud-hosted architectures with authenticated access, database row-level security, encrypted document storage, allow-listed user registration, and immutable change logs — so every edit to a public record is attributable and reviewable.

05

GIS, remote sensing & IoT

Geospatial analysis, remote-sensing assessments, and IoT sensor networks — from groundwater aquifer classification (with the University of Virginia) to solar-powered smart irrigation with live soil-moisture telemetry.

06

Digital capacity building & adoption

Tailor-made training for government staff, field teams, and communities; formation of community structures; user onboarding, help materials, and phased handover — ensuring systems are owned and sustained locally after project close.

Flagship digitization project · Live in production

CIMS — Compensation Information Management System

A full digitization of asset registration, verification, valuation, and compensation tracking for Project Affected Persons (PAPs) under the Lesotho Lowlands Water Development Project Phase III (LLWDP III) — replacing paper files and manual reconciliation with a single, secure, auditable source of truth.

What CIMS digitizes
Household & asset registry. Beneficiaries, co-owners, land parcels, structures, and communal assets in one linked database.
Verification workflow. Print-ready two-page verification forms with photos and ID documents; field completion writes straight back to the database.
Compensation valuation. A central Rates Master governs land and asset rates; compensation auto-calculates (area × rate + disturbance) — one source of truth, no spreadsheet drift.
Payment tracking. Payment status and payment documents per PAP, with live dashboard counters for management oversight.
Document management. IDs, photos, and payment evidence uploaded from the field to encrypted cloud storage, linked to each record.
Governance & data integrity
Role-based access. Distinct tiers for administrators, community liaison and resettlement officers, field surveyors, and view-only client users.
Approval chain. Ordinary edits flow through an edit-request and approval workflow rather than direct changes to official records.
Live audit trail. Every change is logged and visible in a real-time Change History on each record.
Row-level security. Database-enforced permissions on every table; registration restricted to an authorized email allow-list.
De-duplication. Guided merge workflows resolve duplicate PAP files — including multi-asset households — with a reviewable result before commit.

Technology

React single-page application · PostgreSQL (Supabase) with row-level security and realtime subscriptions · Express API · Cloudflare R2 encrypted object storage · continuous deployment on Render. Delivered with user training, operational handover notes, and live production support.

How we deliver

Our approach to public-sector digitization

Principles proven across our live government systems — the same standards we would bring to the Ministry's digitization project.

Built for the field, not just the office

Offline-first applications that keep working on 2G/3G networks and sync when connectivity returns; GPS, photo, and document capture at the point of service; print-ready official forms where paper is still required.

Security & accountability by design

Role-based access, database row-level security, allow-listed registration, approval workflows for changes to official records, and immutable audit logs — every edit attributable, every record reviewable.

One source of truth

Central master data (rates, categories, registries) drives every calculation and report, eliminating spreadsheet drift and reconciliation disputes between departments, contractors, and communities.

AI where it multiplies capacity

Applied AI through our 4D AI division — advisory chat, AI vision monitoring, and predictive analytics — designed for local languages, low bandwidth, and responsible public-sector use.

Adoption, not just deployment

Community sensitization, staff training, user onboarding, and phased handover with documentation — the change management that determines whether digitization sticks.

Cost-effective & locally sustained

Lean cloud architectures with modest running costs; a flexible staffing model that mobilizes specialist expertise per engagement; systems documented for long-term local operation.
